# S A I Website v1 Brief

## Objective
Build a lean, polished public-facing website for S A I.

This is a version 1 presence website intended to support:
- grant and funding applications
- credibility with funding authorities
- early trust with schools, partners, and parents

This is not a product website.
Do not build any login, dashboard, CMS, booking system, or AI video generation feature.

## Brand name
S A I

## What S A I is
S A I is a UK-based education and social impact initiative.

It is being developed to help children build:
- character
- creativity
- confidence
- responsible AI awareness

The idea is to use storytelling, values-based learning, and guided creative technology in an after-school or enrichment setting.

## Core positioning
S A I helps children build character, creativity and confidence through storytelling and responsible AI.

## Target audiences
Primary audience:
- funding authorities
- grant assessors
- foundations
- early funding partners

Secondary audience:
- schools
- parents
- community partners

## Website goal
The website should feel credible, clear, calm, and purposeful.

A visitor should immediately understand:
- what S A I is
- why it exists
- who it is for
- that it is being built responsibly
- that safeguarding and careful AI use are central to the model

## Website structure
The website should include only 3 main pages:

1. Home
2. About
3. Safeguarding & Responsible AI

A simple Contact link or contact section can be included, but the core site should remain minimal.

## Design direction
The site should feel:
- modern
- minimal
- trustworthy
- warm
- educational
- grant-ready

Avoid:
- playful childish design
- startup hype
- flashy animation
- tech-heavy product language
- clutter
- exaggerated claims

## Technical direction
Use:
- Next.js
- TypeScript
- Tailwind CSS

Build a simple static marketing site with clean reusable components.

No backend is needed.

## Copy style
Use UK English.

Tone should be:
- clear
- thoughtful
- credible
- concise
- mission-led

Avoid:
- doom language
- moral panic language
- overclaiming impact
- fake traction
- fake testimonials
- fake logos
- invented partnerships

## Required navigation
- Home
- About
- Safeguarding & Responsible AI
- Contact

## Page requirements

### 1. Home
The Home page should include these sections:

#### Hero
Include:
- headline
- subheading
- primary CTA
- secondary CTA

Use this message direction:
Headline:
Helping children build character, creativity and confidence through storytelling and responsible AI

Subheading:
S A I is a UK-based education and social impact initiative developing after-school enrichment experiences for children. Our approach combines values-based learning, storytelling, creative expression and responsible AI awareness in a format designed for schools, families and communities.

CTA ideas:
- Partner with S A I
- Enquire about pilot opportunities

#### Intro / Why it matters
Explain that children are growing up in a changing digital world and need both values and future-facing confidence.

#### What we do
Explain that S A I is developing a values-led enrichment model where children:
- explore core values
- discuss stories and choices
- build confidence through creative expression
- engage with AI-supported tools in a guided and age-appropriate way

#### Why now
Explain that the future will involve AI and digital tools, and children should be prepared to engage with them thoughtfully and responsibly.

#### Trust / Principles
Include a short section showing that S A I is being built around:
- safeguarding
- inclusion
- responsible AI use
- transparency
- child-centred design

#### Closing CTA
Invite contact from:
- funders
- pilot schools
- community partners
- early supporters

---

### 2. About
The About page should include these sections:

#### Intro
Explain what S A I is and why it exists.

#### Founding idea
Communicate that S A I was created from the belief that children need both:
- strong values
- future-facing skills

#### Why storytelling
Explain that storytelling helps children explore:
- honesty
- kindness
- fairness
- courage
- responsibility
- empathy
- identity
- self-expression

#### Why responsible AI
Explain that S A I is interested in helping children engage with emerging technology in a way that is thoughtful, creative, and grounded.

#### Inclusion and confidence
Include the point that S A I has a strong interest in helping girls and underrepresented young people see themselves as creators and future leaders in technology.

#### Mission and vision
Use these blocks:

Mission:
To help children build character, creativity and confidence through storytelling and responsible AI.

Vision:
A future where children, especially girls and underrepresented young people, can engage with emerging technology as thoughtful, ethical and confident creators.

---

### 3. Safeguarding & Responsible AI
This page should feel especially trustworthy and calm.

It should include these sections:

#### Intro
State clearly that safeguarding is central to the S A I model and not an afterthought.

#### Safeguarding approach
Explain that S A I is being developed with a safeguarding-first mindset and is centred on:
- child wellbeing
- age-appropriate experiences
- adult guidance
- clear boundaries
- inclusive participation
- safe practice

#### Responsible AI approach
Explain that technology is intended to support creativity and reflection, not replace children's thinking or voice.

Include principles such as:
- age-appropriate use
- human creativity first
- careful tool selection
- avoiding unnecessary personal data
- transparency
- critical thinking over passive use
- fairness and inclusion

#### Privacy and transparency
Explain that schools, families, and partners should understand:
- what tools are used
- how they are used
- why they are used

#### Closing statement
End with a child-centred message about building safe, thoughtful, inspiring opportunities for children.

## Contact
Include a simple contact method in the header, footer, or final section.
Use placeholder details if needed, but structure the site so real contact details can be dropped in easily.

## Content constraints
Do not invent:
- statistics
- partnerships
- school pilots
- testimonials
- logos
- certifications
- endorsements

Use clean placeholder design and real, grounded copy only.

## Visual guidance
Use a restrained visual style:
- soft neutral background
- strong typography
- clean spacing
- subtle cards or panels
- accessible contrast
- minimal illustration use

The site should look polished enough to share with a funder, even if the organisation is still early-stage.

## Definition of done
The final result should be:
- responsive
- visually polished
- easy to navigate
- credible for funding applications
- simple to edit later
- clearly focused on mission, trust, and safeguarding
