# SAI Approved Messaging

## One-line description
SAI helps children build character, creativity and confidence through storytelling and responsible AI.

## Short description
SAI is a UK-based education and social impact initiative developing after-school enrichment experiences for children. Our approach combines values-based learning, storytelling, creative expression and responsible AI awareness in a format designed for schools, families and communities.

## Mission
To help children develop character, creativity and responsible AI skills through storytelling.

## Vision
A future where children, especially girls and underrepresented young people, can engage with emerging technology as thoughtful, confident and ethical creators.

## Problem statement
Children are growing up in a world shaped by digital media and AI, but many schools and families still need safe, engaging and age-appropriate ways to introduce these tools meaningfully. At the same time, schools continue to value character, confidence and personal development.

## Solution statement
SAI creates structured enrichment experiences where children explore values such as honesty, kindness, fairness, courage and responsibility, then turn those themes into guided creative story outputs supported by responsible AI use.

## What makes SAI different
- values-led
- storytelling-based
- creative and future-facing
- safeguarding-aware
- focused on confidence and inclusion
- includes a girls' confidence and leadership strand

## Girls' strand
Our girls' confidence and leadership strand helps girls see themselves not only as users of technology, but as storytellers, creators and future leaders in it.

## Safeguarding wording
SAI is committed to a safeguarding-first, age-appropriate and transparent approach. We believe children should be introduced to digital tools thoughtfully, with strong adult guidance, clear boundaries and respect for privacy.

## For schools
SAI is designed to support personal development, creative confidence and responsible digital awareness through a structured after-school model.

## For funders
SAI is building an early-stage social impact model at the intersection of education, creativity, inclusion and responsible AI literacy.
