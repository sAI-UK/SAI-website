# SAI Website Instructions

## Project identity
SAI is a UK-based education and social impact startup.
We help children build character, creativity, confidence, and responsible AI literacy through storytelling.
Our initial offer is an after-school enrichment club for schools and community settings.

The programme combines:
- values education
- storytelling
- creative digital expression
- responsible AI awareness
- a girls' confidence and leadership strand

## What this repository is for
This repo is for the public-facing website only.
The goal is to establish credibility for schools, funders, and parents.
This is not a product app.
Do not build authentication, dashboards, payments, or video generation workflows unless explicitly asked.

## Primary goals of the website
1. Establish trust and legitimacy
2. Explain the mission clearly
3. Show the programme and intended impact
4. Reassure visitors on safeguarding, privacy, and responsible AI use
5. Capture interest from schools, funders, and pilot partners

## Primary audiences
- grant funders
- school leaders
- teachers and programme leads
- parents and carers
- potential partners and sponsors

## Brand tone
Use UK English.
Tone should be:
- warm
- trustworthy
- modern
- educational
- hopeful
- impact-driven
- clear and concise

Avoid:
- hype
- fear-based messaging
- doom language about children or society
- exaggerated claims
- jargon-heavy AI language

## Core positioning
SAI helps children build character, creativity and confidence through storytelling and responsible AI.

SAI is not about replacing learning with AI.
Children do the thinking; technology helps them express and visualise ideas.

## Messaging priorities
Emphasise:
- character and personal development
- creative confidence
- safe and age-appropriate AI exposure
- inclusion and girls' confidence in creative technology
- partnerships with schools and funders

## Required pages
- Home
- About / Mission
- Programmes
- Impact
- Safeguarding & Responsible AI
- Partners / Funders
- Contact

## Homepage priorities
The homepage should clearly answer:
- What is SAI?
- Who is it for?
- Why does it matter now?
- What does the club/programme do?
- Why should schools or funders trust it?
- How can someone get in touch?

## Product constraints
For this prototype:
- static marketing site only
- no backend required
- no CMS required
- no AI video generation integration
- no user login
- no complex forms unless asked
- use placeholder images/illustrations if needed
- optimise for fast iteration and clean presentation

## Recommended build approach
Prefer a clean modern frontend stack suitable for fast prototyping.
Use:
- Next.js
- TypeScript
- Tailwind CSS

Keep the codebase simple and easy to modify.

## Design direction
Visual style should feel:
- credible enough for funders
- friendly enough for parents
- modern enough to signal AI literacy
- not childish
- not corporate and cold

Use:
- soft spacing
- strong headings
- simple cards
- clean sections
- accessible contrast
- mobile-first responsive layout

## Accessibility and UX
- semantic HTML
- accessible buttons and navigation
- responsive design
- readable line lengths
- clear call-to-action buttons
- no cluttered layouts

## Copy rules
Use concise, polished copy.
Prefer plain English over buzzwords.
Keep claims grounded.
Do not invent partnerships, pilots, testimonials, statistics, safeguarding certifications, or funders.

## Content blocks to support
The site should make room for:
- mission statement
- programme overview
- values taught
- girls' confidence and leadership strand
- safeguarding approach
- responsible AI principles
- call for pilot schools and funding partners

## Definition of done for v1
A complete, polished, responsive website that includes:
- a strong homepage
- at least 5 additional content sections/pages
- clear CTA buttons
- placeholder content that can be swapped later
- a visual identity that looks credible for grant applications

## Working style
When implementing:
- make reasonable decisions without blocking on small ambiguities
- keep components modular
- use reusable layout sections
- prefer clarity over cleverness
- leave code easy for a non-technical founder to update later
