UK education and social impact initiative

Helping children build character, creativity and confidence through storytelling and responsible AI

SAI is a UK-based education and social impact initiative developing after-school enrichment experiences for children. Our approach combines values-based learning, storytelling, creative expression and responsible AI awareness in a format designed for schools, families and communities.

Partner with SAIEnquire about pilot opportunities

Why it matters

Children are growing up in a changing digital world. They need strong values and future-facing confidence, with adults helping them engage thoughtfully rather than passively with new tools.

Built for

Funding authorities, grant partners, schools, parents and community organisations looking for a calm, credible and responsible model.

What we do

A values-led enrichment model built around story, reflection and guided creativity.

SAI is being developed to help children explore core values, discuss stories and choices, build confidence through creative expression and engage with AI-supported tools in an age-appropriate way.

Explore core values through guided discussion

Discuss stories, choices and consequences

Why now

The future will involve AI and digital tools. Children should be prepared to engage with them thoughtfully and responsibly.

SAI takes a calm view of this moment. The aim is not to add hype, but to help children build judgement, confidence and creative agency with strong adult guidance.

A clear principle

Children do the thinking. Technology supports creativity and reflection.

That principle shapes the programme concept, the safeguarding stance and the way SAI speaks to schools, families and funding partners.

Trust and principles

SAI is being built around credibility, care and child-centred design.

Visitors should be able to see clearly that safeguarding and careful AI use are not side notes. They are central to the model.
