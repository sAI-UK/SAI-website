Safeguarding & Responsible AI

Safeguarding is central to the SAI model, not an afterthought.

This page is designed to communicate a calm, trustworthy approach for schools, families and funding partners. SAI is being developed with child wellbeing, age-appropriate practice and careful use of technology at its core.

Contact SAI

Safeguarding approach

A safeguarding-first mindset from the beginning

  • Child wellbeing remains central to activity design.
  • Experiences are intended to be age-appropriate and clearly guided.
  • Adult oversight, clear boundaries and safe practice matter throughout.
  • Inclusive participation is part of responsible delivery.

A core principle

Technology is intended to support creativity and reflection, not replace children's thinking or voice.

Responsible AI approach

Careful use, clear boundaries and critical thinking over passive use.

Age-appropriate use with clear adult guidance

Human creativity first, with technology in a supporting role

Careful tool selection and moderated practice

Avoiding unnecessary personal data wherever possible

Transparency about what is used, how and why

Critical thinking over passive use

Fairness, inclusion and thoughtful participation

Privacy and transparency

Schools, families and partners should understand what tools are used, how they are used and why they are used.

SAI aims to keep communication clear and grounded. That includes careful tool choices, straightforward explanation, and an approach that avoids unnecessary personal data wherever possible.

Trust is part of the offer.

For SAI, responsible practice is inseparable from the educational idea itself. The aim is to give adults confidence as well as give children meaningful creative opportunities.
